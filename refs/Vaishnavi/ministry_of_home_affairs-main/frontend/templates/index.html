🏛️ Alignment with Indian Law Enforcement Initiatives
  • CCTNS (NCRB): Ingests FIRs and crime records across stations for unified relational linkage.
  • OCND (NIA & NATGRID): Surfaces cross-state links where the same suspect, vehicle, or phone appears in multiple jurisdictions.
  • I4C (Cyber Crime Coordination Centre): NLP text extraction, mule account clustering, and cyber scam classification.
  • SIMBA / Crime GPT: Fast suspect profiling, network visualization, and multi-hop connection queries.
🔬 AI, NLP & Graph-ML Engine Components
  • Entity Extraction (NER): Regex + Pattern token extraction for Indian phones, vehicles, bank accounts, and aliases.
  • Entity Resolution: Levenshtein & Jaro-Winkler fuzzy deduplication with human-in-the-loop merge controls.
  • Centrality Algorithms: Degree, Betweenness (broker detection), Closeness, and PageRank (kingpin identification).
  • Louvain Modularity: Partitioning of network into distinct operational gangs/cells.
  • Link Prediction: Adamic-Adar & Jaccard index scoring for hidden accomplice discovery.
